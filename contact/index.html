Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?+

Most complete inquiries receive an initial response within one business day.

What is the typical lead time?+

Lead time depends on system scale, configuration, documentation and destination. A project-specific schedule is included with the proposal.

Can I get a quotation without detailed data?+

Yes. Share the information you have and our team will identify the details needed to prepare an appropriate quotation.

Can I visit your factory or test center?+

Factory visits, remote reviews and test-center discussions can be arranged with the responsible sales engineer.

Do you offer test spray services?+

Laboratory and pilot test directions are available after the material and project objective have been reviewed.

Do you support customization?+

Yes. Capacity, atomization, collection, controls, materials, utilities and plant interfaces can be reviewed around the project.

What information helps with a recommendation?+

Material name, feed form, solvent basis, available quantity, target powder, capacity and project stage are especially useful.

How do I become a distributor or partner?+

Select Partnership in the inquiry type and include your market, company profile and intended cooperation scope.
