Frequently Asked Questions

What types of materials can be spray dried?+

Solutions, emulsions and suitable suspensions may be evaluated. Final suitability depends on composition, solids, viscosity, solvent basis and target powder.

How do you protect heat-sensitive materials?+

Atomization, inlet and outlet temperatures, residence time and rapid collection are reviewed together to limit unnecessary thermal exposure.

Can you handle flammable solvents?+

Only after a complete safety review. Suitable projects may require closed-loop operation, inerting, solvent recovery and explosion protection.

What particle sizes can you achieve?+

The practical range depends on the feed, atomizer, process conditions and equipment scale. Testing confirms the achievable distribution.

How do you ensure scale-up success?+

Laboratory and pilot data are connected with atomization, airflow, residence time, collection and production-duty requirements.

Can you evaluate an unfamiliar material?+

Yes. Share the available composition, feed properties and powder targets so our application engineers can review feasibility and the appropriate test route.

What information is needed before testing?+

Useful information includes feed form, solids content, viscosity, solvent, heat sensitivity, target moisture, particle size and expected processing capacity.

Can spray drying improve powder flowability?+

Spray drying can influence particle shape, density, moisture and size distribution. Testing is used to confirm how those changes affect the specific material.

Do you support laboratory and production scales?+

Yes. OLLITAL supports laboratory evaluation, pilot validation and industrial system engineering, with each step matched to the evidence the project needs.

How do I request an application recommendation?+

Submit the material-evaluation form with your feed information, powder target and capacity requirement. An application engineer will review the next step.
