Questions Before You Test

Frequently Asked Questions

What particle-size range can spray drying achieve?+

A broad range is possible, but the practical target depends on material properties, atomizer type, solids content, viscosity and process conditions.

How is particle-size consistency controlled?+

Feed preparation, atomization energy, droplet formation, temperature, airflow and collection are controlled as one process.

Can you control morphology as well as size?+

Yes. Hollow or dense particles, surface texture and agglomeration behavior may be influenced through formulation and process design.

Do you provide pilot testing?+

Yes. Pilot trials can compare atomization directions and operating windows before scale-up.

Which analytical methods are available?+

Depending on the project, review may include laser diffraction, sieving, microscopy, moisture, bulk density and flowability.

Can a particle-size result be scaled up?+

Pilot data helps reduce risk, but final scale-up also requires review of atomizer geometry, air distribution, residence time and collection behavior.
