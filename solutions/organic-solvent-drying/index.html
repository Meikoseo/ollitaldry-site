Questions Before You Test

Frequently Asked Questions

Which organic solvents can be reviewed?+

Ethanol, isopropanol, acetone, ethyl acetate and other solvents may be considered after a complete material, compatibility and safety review.

How are flammable-solvent risks managed?+

The direction may require a closed loop, nitrogen inerting, oxygen monitoring, solvent recovery, pressure protection, suitable electrical classification and interlocks.

Can you design for an ATEX zone?+

ATEX and IECEx requirements can enter the engineering scope. The final protection concept depends on the solvent, concentration, site and destination standards.

Do you provide pilot testing?+

Testing may be possible in a suitably reviewed closed-loop system. The solvent and all hazards must be disclosed before acceptance.

What solvent recovery can be expected?+

Recovery depends on solvent properties, feed load, condenser duty and operating conditions. The target is confirmed during process engineering.

What affects project lead time?+

Safety review, testing, process complexity, compliance documentation and custom fabrication all affect the project schedule.
