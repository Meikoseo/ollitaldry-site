Answers Before You Start

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need for testing?+

Share the material composition, feed form, solids, viscosity when known, available sample amount and target powder properties.

Will my formulation be kept confidential?+

Project information is handled for technical evaluation only. An NDA can be discussed when required.

How long does the testing process take?+

Timing depends on sample readiness, safety review, test scope and equipment availability. A schedule is agreed before testing.

Can you test multiple formulations?+

Yes. A structured test matrix can compare formulations or operating conditions when sufficient material is available.

What sample size is required?+

The amount depends on the selected laboratory or pilot equipment, expected feed rate and required run duration.

What happens after testing?+

Results are reviewed with the next decision in mind: formulation refinement, further pilot validation, equipment selection or production engineering.

Can you test heat-sensitive materials?+

Yes. The review considers thermal sensitivity, target outlet conditions, residence time and collection speed before a suitable test window is proposed.

Can organic-solvent formulations be evaluated?+

Potential solvent projects require a separate safety and compatibility review. Closed-loop, inerting and solvent-recovery requirements are evaluated before testing.

Which powder properties can be measured?+

The available scope may include residual moisture, particle-size distribution, morphology, bulk density, flowability, solubility and product yield.

How are test parameters transferred to a larger dryer?+

Scale-up connects feed behavior and atomization with airflow, thermal balance, residence time, collection efficiency and the required production duty.

Can I observe the test or review results remotely?+

Project review arrangements can include scheduled on-site attendance, remote discussions and engineer-led result reviews, subject to the agreed test plan.

Does a successful test guarantee production performance?+

Testing reduces uncertainty and defines an evidence-based process window. Final production performance also depends on equipment configuration, utilities, controls and operating discipline.
