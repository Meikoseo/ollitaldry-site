Frequently Asked Questions

How can I request a custom resource or documentation?+

Send the topic, equipment model and intended use through the material-evaluation form so the appropriate technical information can be reviewed.

Do you offer on-site or virtual training?+

Training can be included in project scope or discussed separately for operators and maintenance teams.

How do I access published video resources?+

Published recordings appear in the Videos section. Contact us when a recording is not publicly listed.

Can I speak with an expert about my application?+

Yes. Share your material and process goal so the inquiry can be routed to an application engineer.

Which brochure should I request for my project?+

Start with the Product Catalog for an overview, or request the Pilot & Scale-Up Solutions catalog for development work. Include your material and target capacity so our team can suggest relevant information.

What information helps you recommend a spray drying solution?+

Share your material, feed form, target processing capacity and desired powder properties. Known solids content, solvent type and heat sensitivity help our engineers review the application.

Can I request material testing before selecting equipment?+

Submit a material-evaluation request with your sample details and testing goals. Our team will review feasibility and discuss the test scope, sample requirements and next steps.

Are the specifications in your resources suitable for every material?+

Published information is a reference for initial evaluation. Actual performance and the recommended configuration depend on feed properties, process conditions and testing results; confirm project requirements with our engineering team.

How often will I receive resource updates?+

Updates are sent when useful technical articles, videos, brochures or case studies are published. We focus on relevant engineering information rather than frequent promotional messages.

Can I stop receiving resource updates?+

Yes. Reply to any update or contact OLLITAL using the email address you subscribed with, and the address will be removed from future resource communications.
