Answers Before You Start

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects does OLLITAL deliver?+

Projects range from laboratory and pilot systems to industrial spray drying plants, solvent-recovery systems and custom integrated lines.

Can you handle solvent recovery projects?+

Yes, after a complete material, process and safety review. Closed-loop drying, inerting and condensation may enter the project scope.

How long does a typical project take?+

Lead time depends on engineering, testing, fabrication, documentation, destination and site-readiness requirements.

Do you support retrofits of existing spray dryers?+

Existing systems can be reviewed for controls, collection, heat recovery, filtration, spare parts and process optimization opportunities.

Do you provide turnkey solutions?+

The agreed scope can cover process engineering, equipment supply, utilities coordination, installation support, commissioning and training.

What kind of after-sales support is available?+

Support can include remote troubleshooting, spare parts, maintenance planning, operator guidance and process review.

Can a project begin with laboratory testing?+

Yes. Laboratory evaluation can establish feasibility, operating windows and powder targets before pilot or production-scale engineering begins.

How do you select the appropriate dryer capacity?+

Capacity is assessed from feed properties, solids content, evaporation demand, operating hours, target moisture and the intended production plan.

Can OLLITAL support site layout and utility planning?+

Yes. Project engineering can include equipment arrangement, access requirements and coordination of electrical, heating, air and other utility interfaces.

Are project systems customized for local standards?+

Applicable electrical, documentation, material, safety and compliance requirements are reviewed during technical definition and confirmed in the agreed scope.

Is operator training included after installation?+

Commissioning support can include operating procedures, cleaning guidance, routine checks and practical training for the customer team.

How can I request a similar project configuration?+

Use the project inquiry button and provide your material, target capacity and process requirements. An engineer will review the appropriate next step.
