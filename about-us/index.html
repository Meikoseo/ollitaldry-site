Who We Are

Engineered for Performance. Built on Partnership.

OLLITAL designs and delivers spray drying solutions that help customers develop better products, improve efficiency and remain competitive in demanding markets.

Laboratory research, pilot validation, equipment engineering, manufacturing and long-term support are connected through one practical project team.

  • Application-led engineering and repeatable performance
  • Process expertise across diverse feed materials
  • Quality, integrity and long-term cooperation
  • Global project communication with local responsiveness
