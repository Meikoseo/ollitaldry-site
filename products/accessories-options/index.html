FAQ

Accessories & Compatibility Questions

How do I confirm whether an accessory fits my spray dryer?+

Share the model, serial information, current process configuration, material and intended improvement. An engineer will confirm mechanical, electrical and process compatibility.

Can an existing OLLITAL system be upgraded?+

Many systems can accept selected nozzle, collection, monitoring, cleaning and spare-parts upgrades. Compatibility depends on the original model and control architecture.

Are accessories available for non-OLLITAL equipment?+

Some process components can be evaluated, but dimensions, interfaces, controls and performance responsibilities must be reviewed before compatibility is confirmed.

Do accessories require installation support?+

Simple replacement parts may be installed by qualified site personnel. Process, control, safety and recovery upgrades usually require engineering guidance or commissioning.

How should I choose between a cyclone and a filter?+

Selection depends on particle size, powder value, product loss, containment, cleaning needs and process-air conditions. The collection train is reviewed as a whole.

Can I order a preventive-maintenance spare-parts kit?+

Yes. A model-specific kit can include seals, gaskets, filters, sensors and other routine wear parts based on operating duty.

What information is needed for an accessory quotation?+

Provide the dryer model, serial number when available, current configuration, material, operating conditions and the improvement you want to achieve.

Can several upgrades be installed as one package?+

Yes. Compatible atomization, collection, controls, cleaning and safety options can be coordinated into one engineering scope and installation plan.

Will an upgrade change the system capacity?+

Some upgrades improve stability, recovery or operating range, but capacity depends on the complete heat and mass balance. It is reviewed rather than assumed.

Are food-grade and pharmaceutical contact materials available?+

Product-contact material, finish, sealing and documentation options can be reviewed for hygienic duties and the applicable project requirements.

How are electrical and control upgrades matched to my plant?+

Voltage, frequency, control architecture, I/O, communication protocol and site standards are confirmed before the control package is released.

How long does accessory engineering and delivery take?+

Timing depends on compatibility review, customization and component availability. Standard replacement parts are usually faster than engineered process upgrades.
