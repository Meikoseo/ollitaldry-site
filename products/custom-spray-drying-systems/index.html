FAQ

Custom Spray Drying System Questions

How is a custom spray drying system different from a standard model?+

A custom system is engineered around the feed, powder target, production duty, utilities, building constraints, cleaning strategy and destination-market requirements rather than a fixed catalog configuration.

Do you provide laboratory and pilot testing?+

Yes. Representative testing can be used to review feasibility, powder behavior and scale-up direction before the final production system is confirmed.

Can you handle solvent-based or combustible feeds?+

Potentially, after a formal material and safety review. Closed-loop recovery, inerting, explosion protection, filtration and special contact materials may be required.

What information is needed to start?+

Share the feed composition, solids content, viscosity, temperature sensitivity, target capacity, powder properties, utilities, installation site and applicable standards.

Can the system be integrated into an existing plant?+

Yes. Existing upstream and downstream equipment, plant controls, access limits, utilities and future expansion can enter the engineering scope.

How long does a custom project take?+

Timing depends on testing, process complexity, documentation, fabrication and site coordination. A project schedule is provided after the engineering scope is defined.
