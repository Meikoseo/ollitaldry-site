FAQ

Industrial Spray Dryer Questions

What capacity classes are treated as industrial spray dryers?+

OLLITAL treats 50-100+ L systems as industrial-scale equipment. Final evaporation and powder-output performance is calculated from the feed, solids content, heat source and target moisture.

Can the system run continuously?+

Yes. Industrial systems can be configured for continuous production with coordinated feeding, drying, separation, powder transfer and automated controls.

Can heat-sensitive products be processed?+

Yes, after material review. Atomization, air temperatures, residence time and collection strategy are selected around thermal sensitivity and powder targets.

Is cGMP or food-oriented construction available?+

Hygienic construction, documentation, cleaning access and cGMP-oriented options can be reviewed for the intended product and destination market.

How is powder quality kept consistent?+

Feed preparation, atomization, inlet and outlet conditions, residence time, separation and process controls are reviewed as one production system.

Can the system be integrated with an existing plant?+

Yes. Utility interfaces, building constraints, upstream feeding, downstream powder handling and plant controls can enter the engineering scope.

What information is needed for a proposal?+

Share the material, feed state, solids content, target capacity, target moisture, powder properties, utilities, operating schedule and installation location.

Do you provide installation and commissioning?+

Installation guidance, commissioning, operator training, spare parts and remote engineering support can be included in the project scope.

Can solvent-based or combustible feeds be processed?+

These feeds require a formal process and safety review. Inerting, closed-loop recovery, explosion protection, filtration and special materials may be required.

How is the final system size confirmed?+

The final design is confirmed from material data, representative testing when needed, mass and heat balance, utility review and plant-layout coordination.
